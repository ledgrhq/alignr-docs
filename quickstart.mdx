## Start with workspace setup

Creating an account opens the setup wizard. You can skip any step and return from **Settings → Setup**. The help icon opens this documentation.

You are signed in as soon as account creation succeeds. Alignr also queues a welcome email with a **Set up your workspace** link to [the setup wizard](https://app.alignr.io/setup). You do not need to verify your email before starting. If the email has not arrived, open the wizard directly after signing in.

1. **Connect Microsoft:** choose Direct or Partner Center and save the default method. Direct access uses each client administrator’s approval. For Partner Center, connect your MSP’s partner account; customer consent and GDAP relationships remain separate requirements.
2. **Connect your PSA:** choose ConnectWise Manage, HaloPSA or Autotask to connect client and service records.
3. **Connect your RMM:** choose Datto RMM or NinjaOne for device observations. ConnectWise Automate is shown as unavailable. Match the source sites or organisations to the right clients and check the first collection.
4. **Invite teammates:** invite people manually, or preview your MSP’s own Microsoft directory and review each invitation and Alignr role. Previewing a directory does not create users or send invitations. Customer users and guests are not imported as MSP teammates.
5. **Set up standards:** choose a domain card to open its dedicated setup page, then answer one question at a time. Include only checks that fit your policy, connect relevant tools and review the full selection. The guided standard is active when saved, with no clients assigned; deploy it to the intended clients before running checks. Add Devices, Backup or Email & domains later as separate standards.

Alignr prepares secure connection storage automatically. If preparation is still running, continue with another step; the connection form becomes available when protection has been verified. You never need to provision an AWS key yourself.

If setup needs attention, a workspace administrator can use **Retry setup** on the connection step or contact [Alignr support](mailto:support@alignr.io). Your account and the other setup steps remain available.

Completing or skipping wizard steps does not mean your clients have passed their checks. Use **Review setup readiness** to inspect actual connection health and assessment coverage.

For a walkthrough of each setup step, follow [Use the setup wizard](/guides/setup-wizard). For ongoing administration, see [workspace settings](/guides/settings) and [Workspace health](/guides/workspace-health).

## Before you begin

Sign in to [Alignr](https://app.alignr.io) with a workspace account. You need permission to manage integrations, clients and standards. Have the source system’s credentials or an authorised administrator available.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a source">
    Open **Integrations → Add connection**, choose your source and follow its connection form in the dialog. Use the permissions requested by that connector. A successful connection establishes access; it does not mean a client has passed an assessment.

    [Learn about integrations](/guides/integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your clients">
    Open **Clients → Import clients** for records from your connected Microsoft, PSA or RMM source. Link each record to the right existing client, or explicitly create a new client. Check names and identifiers before confirming the mapping. Importing a client and collecting device evidence are separate checkpoints.

    [Understand Organizations](/guides/organizations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your standard">
    On **Standards**, select **Create standard**, then choose a guided domain. You can also choose **Choose a domain** in workspace setup. A guided domain standard is active when saved, but starts with no clients assigned. In its details, choose **Deploy to clients** and save the client assignments; this does not run checks. New blank/general standards, library copies and checklist imports remain disabled and workspace-wide by default; existing standards keep their saved scope.

    Review and enable a disabled workspace-wide draft with **Review & enable** only after checking its scope, controls, parameters, evidence requirements and client-specific overrides. Confirm the standard is enabled before running checks.

    [Choose a baseline](/controls/baselines/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review collected evidence">
    Check that collection has completed for the intended client. Look at source and observation time. Missing or stale evidence must not be interpreted as passing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the assessment">
    After assigning clients to a guided standard, select **Run checks** from its details or **Standards**. Review the standard, selected clients and settings, then confirm **Run checks for N clients**. Deployment sets which clients the standard applies to; running checks is a separate assessment. Wait for the evaluation to complete before interpreting the results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the results">
    Open the client’s alignment results. Investigate failures, distinguish missing data from unsupported coverage, and assign manual checks where needed. Review a supported remediation plan before approving a change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What success looks like

You can identify the client, the standard being assessed, and the evidence behind each result. An incomplete assessment is useful when it makes its gaps visible.

[Understand control statuses](/guides/control-status).

## A worked first assessment

Use a copy of the **BIOS Identity Assurance** library template after reviewing its draft controls and connecting a source that supplies the necessary observations.

1. Confirm the source's directory maps to the intended client.
2. Inspect an account's **Administrator role** observation (`has_role`) and its source.
3. Inspect the same account's **MFA registered** observation (`mfa_registered`).
4. Review the administrator MFA control and its selected population.
5. Read the result: an observed `false` can prove failure; a missing observation cannot prove a pass.

This checks registration, not every aspect of MFA enforcement. The [control walkthrough](/guides/control-conditions) explains the definition and the difference between passing, failing and unknown subjects.

## Learn the language as you go

You do not need to memorise technical names. A **predicate** names what is observed; a **fact** records a value for a subject; a **control** compares evidence with an expectation. Keep the [glossary](/guides/glossary) and [predicate reference](/guides/predicate-reference) available when reviewing unfamiliar results.
