Alignr brings your client environments, standards and evidence together so you can see what needs attention and decide what to do next.

To get started with Alignr, you’ll need:

1. Access to your MSP’s [Alignr workspace](https://app.alignr.io).
2. A client to assess and an integration you can authorise.

Then, connect a source, map its records to your clients, and apply a standard.
