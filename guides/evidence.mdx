## Facts

Integrations normalise observations into facts: a subject, a predicate and a value, together with their Organization, source system, source reference, observation time and confidence.

## Trace a result

When reviewing a finding, check the cited evidence. Confirm that the source describes the intended client and subject, and that its observation is recent enough for the control.

## Missing and stale evidence

No evidence is not evidence of a healthy environment. Missing or stale observations cannot count as passing. Check collection and mapping before assuming a client meets a standard.

Coverage is specific to the evidence a control requires. A connected integration may support some controls and leave others uncovered.

## Agent answers

Alignr’s agent claims require supporting citations. Review the cited source before acting on an answer, especially where a change affects a client’s environment.
