Start at **Overview** to decide what needs attention. Use **Workspace health** for collection and coverage problems, **Issues** for observed findings, and **Reviews** for human assessment work.

## Keep your workspace and client in view

Use the sidebar to move between daily tasks. The top-left workspace menu contains shared settings, plus team members and billing when your role permits. It also contains **Account settings** and **Sign out** for your personal account.

There is no global client filter. Client pages use the client in their route, while workspace pages that support client scope provide their own client controls. Check the page's scope label and controls before acting. Some actions, including **Refresh evidence**, remain workspace-wide even when you arrived from a client page.

Search, **Ask Alignr** when permitted, and the **Colour theme** menu stay in the toolbar. Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark** without leaving your task.

## Read the dashboard together

Overview requires `dashboard.read`. Its **Next step** points towards unfinished setup or assessment work.

| Card | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Live alignment** | What proportion of checks with a current live result are passing? |
| **Checks completed** | What proportion of applicable checks have current live results? |
| **Open issues** | Which observed findings need investigation? |
| **Fix history** | What has been proposed or attempted, and with what outcome? |

Read alignment alongside coverage. In an illustrative example, ten passing results and ten checks without usable evidence do not establish that all twenty expectations are met. **No live checks to score** means clients exist but none has a current verified result to score; **Not assessed yet** appears when there are no clients. Neither label means a score of zero. Each check contributes a pass or fail only when its own required evidence is current, live and from a connected source selected for that check. Missing, stale, simulated or otherwise unverified evidence remains unknown. For example, if four checks have eligible results—two pass and two fail—the score is 50%. An unrelated stale asset still needs attention, but it does not erase those four results or turn the score into a measure of overall client health.

Open a client to see its overview before the tabs. **Current assessment** shows passing checks as a count of eligible assessed checks (for example, **2 of 4 assessed checks passing**) and the active-check coverage count and percentage (for example, **4 of 10 active checks assessed (40%)**). Excluded checks are outside that percentage's denominator. **Check coverage** shows the status bar and count legend, plus the total number of checks. Simulated or unverified results do not count as live passes or failures. A client-level evidence warning describes the wider collection; it does not change a check's result when that check has its own eligible evidence. The chart is a current status distribution, not a history or trend. **Open issues** links to that client's findings when your role can read issues. In **Checks**, **Next steps** is collapsed by default; expand it to see checks needing review and their suggested follow-up. The **Evidence collection** disclosure shows collection and assessment states, source outcomes and any error codes. **Automatic checks** shows the scheduled evaluation status, assessed-control and finding counts, and any error. A warning remains visible when work needs attention.

The overview remains in place while you switch client tabs. When you return to a client or bring its page back into focus, Alignr refreshes stale data; if that refresh fails, the last loaded result stays visible with a warning that it may be out of date. **Tool health** is workspace-wide; open **Workspace health** for detailed collection and coverage checks.

## Refresh evidence deliberately

Select **Refresh evidence** when you need a discovery sweep. This requires both `sweep.execute` and `sweep.read`. It requests workspace collection; opening it from a client page does not turn this action into a single-client sync.

Review the sweep outcome and each source's result. A finished attempt with no facts is not proof of a healthy environment: a source may have failed or supplied no usable observations.

Evidence collection and evaluation are distinct milestones. After confirming the intended evidence is available, use the relevant **Run checks** workflow and inspect its evaluation time. Refreshing the dashboard alone does not establish a fresh assessment.

On a client's page, **Run all checks** queues the enabled automated checks from enabled standards that apply to that client. It runs each standard for this client only and uses evidence already collected; it does not refresh a source or complete manual checks. If evidence is missing or stale, refresh it first, then run checks and review the results in **Checks**.

## Choose your next task

1. **Missing or stale evidence:** open [Workspace health](/guides/workspace-health), fix the source or mapping problem and confirm fresh observations.
2. **An observed failure:** open [Issues](/guides/triage-issues), inspect evidence and decide the follow-up.
3. **A question requiring human judgement:** open **Reviews** or the client's manual checks and record the review evidence.
4. **A proposed or attempted fix:** open **Activity** for approvals and fix history.

**Checkpoint:** the next action has a client, a reason and an owner. A quiet issue queue is useful only alongside an understood coverage picture.

## Find your way around

The sidebar groups daily work—**Overview**, **Issues**, **Reviews** and **Activity**—separately from workspace destinations (**Clients**, **Standards**, **Integrations**) and management (**MCP**, **Settings**). On a narrow screen, open **Open navigation** for the same permitted destinations.

Open **Clients** from the sidebar to find a client by name. Workspace pages that support a client scope expose their own client controls and URL state; changing one page does not set a global client filter for other pages. If a page offers an all-clients option, use it to return to the workspace-wide view.

A page reached from a specific client or control may carry its own explicit client filter. Check the page's scope labels and any client controls before interpreting counts or asking the assistant to explain them. Client scope is page-specific and is not carried over as a global workspace selection.

Press **⌘K** on macOS or **Ctrl+K** to open search. It offers page shortcuts immediately and searches clients and issues after at least two characters. Use the arrow keys and Enter to open a result. This app search is separate from the documentation site's search.

The utility bar provides search, **Ask Alignr** and theme switching. The top-left workspace menu includes **Account settings** and **Sign out** alongside workspace actions. Open help from the bottom of the desktop sidebar or **Documentation** in mobile navigation. Permission-dependent pages and actions may be hidden.

## Inspect Activity and export audit history

Open **Activity** to choose:

| Destination | Purpose | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Approvals** | Review proposed actions before execution. | `approval.read` to view; `remediation.approve` to decide. |
| **Fix history** | Review runs, verification and supported rollback. | `remediation.read`; `remediation.rollback` to reverse eligible runs. |
| **Audit trail** | Inspect recorded actors, actions and affected records. | `audit.read`. |

The Audit trail destination opens the **Audit trail** page. A closed issue's **view audit trail** action opens its record-specific history.

### Find and order records

Use **Search audit trail** to match part of an action, actor label, record type or client name. Search ignores letter case and treats characters such as `%` and `_` literally. It searches the matching collection, including records beyond the current page; it does not search event metadata.

Combine search with **Actor kind**, **Action**, **Record type**, **Record id**, **From** and **To**. Click **Time**, **Actor** or **Event** to cycle ascending → descending → default order. On a narrow screen, use **Sort audit trail** for the same choices. Default order is newest first. Search, filters and ordering apply before pagination; changing them returns to the first page. Sorting alone does not count as a filter: **Reset order** returns to the default.

### Check the client scope

The page's client scope comes from its URL or its own client control. An explicit client URL travels with a shared link; clearing that page's client filter returns the view to all clients where the page supports that choice. No global client selection is applied.

A client-scoped view includes events associated with that client; events without a client are excluded. Clear the page's client filter to inspect the workspace-wide trail. Check the page scope and URL when reviewing a shared link.

### Export the same view

Select **Export filtered CSV** to export matching records, or **Export CSV** without filters. The file uses the table's effective client scope, search, filters and ordering across the matching collection, including records beyond the displayed page.

Exports currently contain up to **50,000 rows**. If the export message says the row limit was reached, narrow the date range and export the remaining periods. Do not treat a truncated file as the complete trail.

Text that could be interpreted as a spreadsheet formula receives a leading apostrophe in the CSV. For example, `=SUM(1,2)` is exported as `'=SUM(1,2)`. This is a spreadsheet-safety presentation change: stored audit values and structured JSON values remain unchanged. A CSV parser may show the apostrophe explicitly.

Each export is recorded in the audit trail with its row count and selected filters. Raw search text is not retained in that export event; it records whether search was applied. Page filters and ordering remain in the page URL. Exported files should follow your organisation's handling rules for operational records.

## Distinguish alerts from completed work

The workspace-health banner points to underlying operational warnings. Read success and error messages after an action, then confirm the saved state or resulting record. Dismissing a message does not resolve its underlying issue.

If a page shows last-known data after a refresh error, restore access and refresh before describing it as current. Follow [troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting) when an action or result does not match your expectation.
