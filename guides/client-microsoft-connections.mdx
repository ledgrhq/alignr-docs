---
title: "Microsoft connections for each client"
description: "Connect a client directly or link its Microsoft tenant through your Partner Center account."
---

Open **Clients → select client → Connections**. For each assigned Microsoft connection, select **Client sites and access** to inspect which sites supply data to this client and open the tenant-access panel. For a client without a Microsoft connection, choose one of two actions:

| Action | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Link Partner Center connection** | Your saved partner account contains this customer and has the required delegated access. |
| **Add Microsoft connection** | A client administrator will authorise Direct access, including clients outside your partner relationship. |

Both routes connect the client's Microsoft tenant. Your MSP's tenant ID and the client's Alignr Organization ID are not substitutes for that Microsoft tenant ID. Review [Microsoft prerequisites and permissions](/integrations/microsoft-setup) before authorising access.

Viewing requires `integration.read`; setup and access checks require `integration.manage`. You also need access to the client.

## Set your usual workspace connection

Open **Settings → Microsoft** and choose **Default connection method**. For Partner Center, select the saved partner account and choose **Save Microsoft defaults**. Complete **Request admin consent** and **Authorise partner account** if they are outstanding. The setup wizard also saves Partner Center as the workspace default when you connect a partner account there.

The default applies to clients that inherit it. A client with an explicit Direct or Partner Center choice keeps that choice. Saving a default does not establish customer access: the customer assignment, Microsoft consent and delegated roles still need to match.

Alignr's shared Direct and Partner Center routes use separate platform applications. One route's authorisation does not authorise the other route.

## Connect a client directly

Use Direct when the client is outside your partner relationship or needs the supported Direct route for Intune device evidence.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Direct setup">
    In the client's **Connections** tab, select **Add Microsoft connection**. This opens **Connect Microsoft directly** for the current client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify the client tenant">
    Enter the client's **Microsoft tenant ID**. If needed, use the domain lookup and confirm that the result belongs to the intended client.

    Leave **Use my own Microsoft application** off to use the configured Alignr Direct application. For a custom application, enable it and enter **Application client ID** and **Application secret**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare and authorise">
    Select **Prepare direct connection**. Alignr prepares the client's Entra ID and Intune connections, maps the tenant and saves the Direct route.

    In the resulting access panel, select **Request administrator consent**. Have an administrator of the client tenant review and approve Microsoft's request. Preparing the connection alone does not grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and collect">
    Return to the client's **Connections** tab. Open **Client sites and access**. In the **Sites and Microsoft access** dialog, select **Check access**. This checks only the tenant linked to this client.

    Open the relevant integration and select **Collect now**. Inspect **Collection history**, then the client's **Evidence** tab. For device evidence, also review the Intune connection and its collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Checkpoint:** the connection belongs to the intended client tenant, required access is available and current observations are visible before you run the client's checks.

## Link a Partner Center customer

1. Confirm the intended partner account is selected in **Settings → Microsoft** and has completed partner authorisation.
2. In the client's **Connections** tab, select **Link Partner Center connection**. Choose the saved partner account if prompted.
3. Find the Microsoft customer belonging to this client and select **Link to client**. Use **Refresh sites** if its directory needs updating. Customers already assigned elsewhere must be reviewed in the connection's **Client mapping** before moving them.
4. On the client page, select **Check access** to sample the linked customer's current Microsoft capabilities. It checks this client only and does not request consent. The result is an observation of access at check time, not a guarantee that every record can be collected.
5. If consent is missing, use **Connect client** in the Partner access panel. This is the explicit consent action: it checks the assigned customer and can request the missing read consent through your authorised Partner Center account. It uses the existing delegated relationship; it does not create a GDAP relationship or add roles to your partner user. **GDAP** means Microsoft's granular delegated admin privileges. See [Microsoft's introduction](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/customers/gdap-introduction).
6. Open the integration and use **Collect now**. Inspect the new attempt and current client evidence before running checks.

The client-page **Check access** action is read-only and scoped to that client. The integration-page **Check access** action samples the selected customers for that connection. **Connect client** is the named Partner action that can request customer read consent; collection is a separate step. A successful sample for one customer does not prove every customer can be collected.

## Read the result

The partner panel shows completed partner setup separately from customer access. **Check access** reports the capabilities currently available to the client. Use **Connect client** when you intend to request missing read consent. A completed administrator-consent return and a saved partner authorisation are setup milestones; neither proves customer access.

If some reads work and others are unavailable, review the specific missing capability. Licensing, workload availability and GDAP roles can affect it even when app consent has succeeded. Repeating an already accepted, unchanged app grant does not repair those conditions.

Use **Collection history → View details** for the saved failure explanation and available response fields. [Collection troubleshooting](/guides/maintain-integrations#inspect-a-collection-attempt) explains the difference between records read and facts written, and the limits of older attempts without diagnostics.

Partner Graph collection handles each selected customer independently. If at least one customer succeeds and another fails, the attempt is **partial**: fresh facts from successful customers are published, while the failed customer's existing Partner Graph observations remain historical and do not count as current evidence or coverage. The shared collection cursor does not advance, so retry collection after resolving the failed customer's access. If every selected customer fails, the attempt fails. Collection does not change customer consent or GDAP roles. See [Inspect a collection attempt](/guides/maintain-integrations#inspect-a-collection-attempt) for recovery guidance.

## Replace an existing connection

Review the saved client route, tenant and mappings before replacing a connection. A replacement application or tenant requires its own authorisation and fresh evidence. Keep the old connection's history available while verifying the replacement, and follow [integration maintenance](/guides/maintain-integrations) before retiring it.

Direct setup will reject a conflicting saved client tenant or application instead of silently changing it. Resolve the existing client configuration before trying again. Never use an unrelated customer assignment to work around that conflict.

**Finish when:** the intended connection has supplied fresh evidence and the client's assessment can be traced to it. Consent, access checking, collection and assessment are separate milestones.

## Mailbox evidence

Partner Center collects supported identity evidence. Mailbox type, time zone and automatic replies require a Direct Microsoft application connection with mailbox access. The Partner access check keeps mailbox settings unavailable; it does not treat missing mailbox data as passing evidence.
