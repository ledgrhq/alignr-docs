## Standards

A standard groups controls describing the technical posture you expect. Review the controls and their parameters before enabling or applying the standard.

## Controls

A control evaluates evidence against an expectation. Automated checks depend on the facts a connector supplies; manual checks let your team assess conditions that need human confirmation.

## Client overrides

Use a client-specific override when a client’s agreed requirement differs from the global standard. Review the effective parameters for that client rather than assuming the global values still apply.

## Review results

Check the result, supporting evidence and evaluation time together. A connector’s presence alone does not establish coverage, and missing evidence cannot establish a pass.

[Read the status guide](/guides/control-status) before treating results as a complete assessment.
