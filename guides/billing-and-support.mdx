title: "Billing and support"
description: "Review billing connection status, open the billing portal and get help with the right context."
---

Open the top-left workspace menu and select **Billing**, or choose **Settings → Billing**, to review your MSP workspace's billing connection. On a narrow screen, use **Open navigation → Billing**. Billing belongs to the workspace, separately from your personal account. You need `billing.read` to see the page and `billing.manage` to connect a profile or open the billing portal.

Plans and custom subscriptions are arranged separately. Connecting a billing profile does not make a charge. For pricing, an agreement or a billing question, contact [support@alignr.io](mailto:support@alignr.io).

## Connect a billing profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the mode and state">
    Read the Billing section. **Test mode · No live charges** is different from **Live billing**. A test subscription does not establish that a live payment was made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the connection">
    Where offered, select **Connect billing profile**. The confirmation **Billing profile requested** means setup has been requested; wait for the connected state.

    Billing setup does not limit workspace access while it is pending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the connected portal">
    Once the profile is ready, select **Open billing portal**. This takes you to the billing provider's portal for the connected workspace. Review the account and any available subscription information there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Checkpoint:** Billing reports that the profile is connected and an authorised manager can open its portal. This confirms the connection, not a particular plan, charge or payment outcome.

If opening the portal says **Billing portal setup is incomplete**, contact support to finish the setup. If it says **Billing portal is temporarily unavailable**, try again later. Repeating the action will not resolve a setup issue.

## Read the status

| Message | Meaning and next action |
| --- | --- |
| Billing has not been configured | The billing integration is unavailable for this workspace. Contact support if you need it arranged. |
| Connect this workspace to billing | An authorised manager can select **Connect billing profile**. |
| Your billing profile is being connected | Setup is pending. The page checks for an updated state. |
| Billing setup needs attention | Review the message; an authorised manager can select **Retry billing setup**. Workspace access remains available. |
| Your billing profile is connected | Use **Open billing portal**. Plans are managed separately. |
| Billing status is temporarily unavailable | Select **Try again**. A failed status request is not proof of a payment problem. |

The app shows the subscription records received for this workspace, including identifiers and statuses where available. It is a saved billing view, not a fresh provider query on every page load. Confirm invoices, payments and commercial questions through the portal or support rather than inferring them from a stale status.

## Calculate a Standard plan estimate

When billing is configured in test or live mode, anyone with `billing.read` can choose an offer under **Standard plan estimate** and select **Calculate estimate**. The server counts this MSP workspace's non-archived Organizations. It prices at least ten clients, so a workspace with three current clients shows **3 active clients · 10 billed**. Select **Calculate estimate** or **Refresh estimate** after the client count or offer changes; the calculator does not update a displayed amount automatically.

| Offer | USD rate | Estimate period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $29.00 per billed client | Month |
| Annual commitment, paid monthly | $26.10 per billed client | Month |
| Annual upfront | $295.80 per billed client | Year |

For three non-archived clients, the ten-client minimum makes the illustrative subtotals $290.00 per month, $261.00 per month and $2,958.00 per year respectively. These are **pre-tax estimates**, not invoices or final payment amounts. The estimate is checked against the configured test or live Chargebee catalogue, and no customer, checkout, subscription, invoice or charge is created by calculating it. An annual-commit estimate does not establish or enforce a twelve-month agreement; terms and activation are arranged separately. Scale/Enterprise pricing remains quote-only.

If the calculator is absent, billing may not be configured for the workspace or your role may lack `billing.read`. If it says **Estimate unavailable**, select **Try again** later or contact support; do not treat the last displayed amount as current. Workspaces above 100,000 billable clients need a tailored estimate.

## Arrange or change your agreement

Contact [support@alignr.io](mailto:support@alignr.io) to discuss pricing, plan changes, custom subscriptions or cancellation. Include the workspace name and the change you want. For a billing-record query, include the subscription or invoice reference if you have one.

Confirm applicable prices, taxes, billing frequency, notice periods and service terms in your agreement. This guide does not define a pricing tier, refund policy or cancellation entitlement. Connecting a profile is not a substitute for agreeing those terms.

## Get help with a product issue

Start with [troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting) or [Workspace health](/guides/workspace-health) when collection or assessments appear incomplete. If the issue remains, email [support@alignr.io](mailto:support@alignr.io).

Use this outline:

```text
Workspace:
Affected client, if relevant:
Page or workflow:
What I expected:
What happened:
Time and timezone:
Safe error message or record reference:
Steps already tried:
Operational impact:
```

Include the relevant finding, run or subscription identifier when useful. Share a redacted screenshot only if it helps explain the problem. Do not send passwords, API tokens, invitation links, reset links, integration secrets or unnecessary client records.

A missing action can be a role-permission issue; include the page and action rather than sharing another user's credentials. For a lost password or authenticator, follow [sign-in recovery](/guides/sign-in-recovery).

## Confirm the next step

Keep the support conversation linked to the affected task. After a proposed resolution, repeat the original workflow and inspect the saved state or fresh evidence. A successful retry is more useful than assuming the problem cleared because an email was sent.

For response-time commitments, incident escalation or service availability requirements, confirm the terms applicable to your agreement. For security or deletion questions, use [Security and your data](/guides/security-and-data).
