## Three questions before connecting

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Does it discover clients or collect evidence? | IT Glue and Hudu import client names and IDs; they do not populate control facts. |
| Which account, tenant, site or appliance does it represent? | A shared product name does not establish which client owns its data. |
| Does it supply the required observation? | N-central and Syncro currently report management presence; that alone does not supply patch compliance. |

Microsoft also has distinct identity, Intune and subscription connections. Use [client Microsoft settings](/guides/client-microsoft-connections) to choose Direct or Partner access deliberately.
