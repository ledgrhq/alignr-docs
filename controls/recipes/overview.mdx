A good control recipe starts with one question and finishes with evidence you can explain. Use these recipes after the [first-client journey](/journeys/first-assessment), or jump straight to the expectation you need.

| Recipe | Question | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Endpoint encryption | Do MDM-enrolled devices report encryption enabled? | [Build the encryption control](/controls/create-custom). |
| Vulnerability threshold | Is the observed open-finding count within an agreed limit? | [Build a configurable count check](/controls/recipes/vulnerability-limit). |
| Administrator MFA | Do observed Global Administrators have MFA registered? | [Follow the identity assessment](/journeys/read-your-first-result). |
| Backup and recovery | What do protection, job status, recency and a restore test each establish? | [Assess backup and recovery](/controls/recipes/backup-confidence). |

## Read a recipe in four parts

1. **Scope:** which clients and subjects are assessed?
2. **Condition:** which observation is compared with which expectation?
3. **Examples:** what would satisfy, contradict or leave the question unanswered?
4. **Limits:** what requires another control or a manual review?

The simple control editor can create an observation-based population and a single expectation. Exact population values and additional filters require an appropriate built-in definition or an [advanced definition](/controls/advanced-definitions).

## Before enabling a recipe

Confirm source coverage, inspect an editable draft, review client settings and [test the result cases](/controls/test-and-rollout). Sample thresholds are starting points to discuss, not a recommended policy for every client.
